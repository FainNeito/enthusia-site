# Market insights integrity

Requirement: historical stall links resolve only from the recorded stall ID and a matching public map stall. The current owner's name must not be used as a substitute for historical attribution. Stall IDs must not be presented as player accounts. Missing winner item breakdowns are labeled unavailable.

The user selected Eastern Time for weekly winners. A market week starts at midnight Monday in America/New_York; its UTC boundaries change with daylight saving time. The service's selected-week query, weekly history grouping and the browser controls must agree. Added regression cases cover timestamps on both sides of the spring and fall transitions; they are not yet run. SQL historical grouping uses modern US daylight-saving rules (appropriate to the server's 2025 onward history).

The public market map responds with stall IDs such as `stall1`. The insights ledger currently has no exported sales; an older local Market sales producer deliberately emits no historical stall or guild attribution. It can provide aggregate prices but cannot determine the weekly stall or guild rankings. Sale-time attribution requires separate provider work and must not be backfilled by guessing from current ownership.

The newer local Market candidate exports partial solo-sale coverage. The website sync persists a source completeness marker only after the final page, rejects older concurrent status overwrites and treats missing or stale confirmation as incomplete. The UI labels partial rankings and withholds official winners and the weekly-gold layer while coverage is incomplete. These changes require deployment of both the worker and site; neither has been deployed.

Review finding: a fresh completeness flag does not prove that earlier weeks are complete or that a restore created no gaps. The service therefore reports coverage incomplete unconditionally, even for a fresh complete marker. Official winners, hall of fame, streaks and the weekly-gold map layer remain unavailable until a historical coverage and recovery contract is implemented and verified. This is a fail-closed limitation, not a finished sales integration.

Read-only inspection of Market main `57704f1acfaf8aacfdd65cb4ae74c0f36b55712d`: the trade flow has `shop.stallId` and an optional `guildId`, and the event carries `shopId` and `guildId`. `ShopTransactionRecorder` currently discards the guild identifier and does not record a stall. The immutable history outbox currently writes version 1 records. Future attribution therefore needs a compatible event/record change, a versioned outbox reader that retains version 1 compatibility, a database migration and a bounded public projection. Old transactions must remain unattributed unless a historical source proves otherwise. The existing Market checkout and its uncommitted exporter work have not been modified.

The full website suite passed (644 tests), including Eastern week boundaries, DST transitions and attribution. The explicit `market-integrity-demo=1` preview mode uses sample data and does not query or change production sales. Supported mobile-browser acceptance, live provider and production verification are not established. Project-local SPEAR state helpers and an EARS validator were not found in this checkout.
